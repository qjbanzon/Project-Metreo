Notes on the data
  • This page is a copy of the sheet as read on November 9, 2026. It does not refresh when the workbook changes; ask for a refresh after the sheet is updated.
  • On the combined view, the sheet shows CPM and Cost Per Reach as the sum of the four brand values (for example 100.06 + 0.32 + 0.22 + 0.11 = ₱100.70), and Cost Per Lead as their average. Those are not calculated from group totals, so treat all three as unverified.
  • The workbook’s TABLES sheet still labels the 2026 figures “Aug 2026 YTD,” but its “Read First” log has entries dated October 22, so the label may be out of date and the period is unconfirmed. The Master Dashboard FY sheet does not say what period 2025 covers, so “vs 2025” changes may compare a partial year with a full one. The TABLES sheet has a same-period comparison for website users, inquiries, and Meta reach and inquiries.
  • Combined Leads appears to be Salesforce website leads plus Meta leads (5,017 + 25,023 = 30,040). The sheet notes that website leads are in Salesforce and Meta leads are not.
  • The TABLES sheet notes that only 1,107 of Avida’s leads are uploaded to Salesforce, about 5% of its total leads. Most of Avida’s 20,330 leads are Meta leads that are not in Salesforce.
  • Alveo Land’s reach now includes Google Ads (Demand Gen and PMax) reach, per the October 22 entry in the workbook’s “Read First” log, so its reach and group reach are not directly comparable with earlier versions of this page.
  • Ayala Land Premier’s reach now includes Google Ads (Search and Demand Gen/PMax) reach, per the workbook’s “Read First” log, so it is not directly comparable with earlier versions of this page. That log entry is dated “Nov 11,” which is after the date this page was read, so the date may be a typo.
  • Ayala Land Inc. Corporate’s views (303.5M), reach (109.4M), and Meta viewers (108.5M) are far above where they stood before October 27, and its spend rose from ₱1.70M to ₱7.75M in this version. Its leads went down from 752 to 735 even though year-to-date totals should only grow. Verify these entries before presenting.
  • The monthly views come from each brand’s own input sheet (rows “Combined Views,” “Combined Reach,” “Combined Leads,” and “Combined Spend”), and they add up to the year-to-date figures on this page. Avida Land has no figures for October to December, and its September spend is blank even though September reach and leads are logged, so its recent months look lower than they may be.
  • Ayala Land Inc. Corporate has ₱2.22M of spend in each of November and December, with no views, reach, or leads logged for those months. This looks like planned budget, and it is included in Corporate’s year-to-date spend (₱7.75M) and the group total (₱33.08M). About ₱4.44M of the group’s spend figure may therefore be planned rather than spent.
  • Ayala Land Inc. Corporate has no 2025 figures in the sheet, so no year-over-year change is shown for it.
